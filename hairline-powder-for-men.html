What hairline powder does

Hairline powder is a cosmetic, not a treatment. It does not regrow hair and it does not change what is happening on your scalp. What it does is add visual density: fine keratin micro-fibers bond to your existing hair shafts and the surrounding area, so a thinning hairline or a receding corner looks filled in. The effect is immediate and lasts until you wash it out.

MELD is built for the hairline specifically. The built-in applicator lets you place the powder precisely along the front edge and the temples, where coverage has to look natural up close.

Why men use it on the hairline

  • It is fast. Application takes well under a minute once you have the placement down.
  • It is waterproof. The hold is rated for 48 hours and is designed to survive sweat and rain, so it is built to stay put through a workout or a commute.
  • It is reversible. It washes out with any shampoo. Nothing is permanent, and there is no buildup left behind.
  • It is discreet. Orders ship in a plain box with no branding on the outside.

Who it suits

Hairline powder works best when there is still some hair in the area for the fibers to bond to. If you have a thinning or softening hairline, diffuse thinning at the temples, or a receding corner with fine hair remaining, you are a good candidate. If the area is fully bald with no hair at all, fibers have nothing to grip and the result will look less convincing. We would rather tell you that up front than sell you the wrong thing.

If you are unsure, email hello@meldhair.com with a photo of your coverage area and we will tell you straight whether MELD is the right fit before you spend a dollar.

How to apply it to your hairline

1. Start dry

Apply to dry, styled hair. The fibers grip best when the hair is dry.

2. Dab, do not dump

Press the applicator pad into the powder, then tap it gently along the hairline. Build up coverage in light passes rather than one heavy load. Less is more at the front edge.

3. Blend and set

Lightly pat the area with your fingertips to blend the front line. That is it. The hold sets on its own and holds through the day.

Choosing a shade

MELD comes in four shades: Black, Dark Brown, Light Brown, and Blonde. When you are between two shades, go lighter for a more natural, blended look at the hairline. If the shade you pick is not right, the Free Shade Swap covers a replacement at no cost.
