What they have in common

Both Hairo and MELD are cosmetic keratin hair fiber products. They add visual density to existing hair to make thinning areas look fuller. Neither is a hair regrowth treatment, and neither changes what is happening on the scalp. Both are surface cosmetics that wash out. They work best when there is still hair in the area for the fibers to bond to.
