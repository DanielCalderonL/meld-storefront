Both use keratin hair fibers

Toppik and MELD belong to the same category: cosmetic keratin hair fibers that cling to existing hair to make thinning areas look denser. Neither is a regrowth treatment. Both are surface cosmetics that wash out with shampoo. If you already understand how Toppik works, you understand the basic mechanism behind MELD.

The differences come down to format, hold, and the guarantee behind the purchase.

What MELD offers

  • Waterproof 48-hour hold. Built to survive sweat, rain, and humidity without a separate setting spray step.

  • Free Shade Swap. If the shade you choose is not right, we send the correct one at no cost. It is a standing policy.
  • 60-day money-back guarantee. If MELD is not right for you, email us within 60 days for a full refund. No need to ship the jar back.
