What a hair density filler is

A hair density filler is a cosmetic powder that makes existing hair look denser. It is not a regrowth treatment and it makes no claim to change what is happening on your scalp. It works on the surface: fine keratin micro-fibers cling to your hair shafts and the area around them, closing the visual gaps where the scalp would otherwise show. The result is fuller-looking hair that lasts until you shampoo it out.

MELD uses keratin, the same protein hair is made of, so the fibers match the visual weight and texture of real hair rather than sitting on top like a flat film.

Where men use it

  • The crown. The most common spot for diffuse thinning, and the hardest to see yourself. The applicator makes it manageable.
  • Diffuse thinning across the top. Where overall density has dropped but coverage remains.
  • The hairline and temples. Where a softening front edge stands out most in photos and good light.

It works across straight, wavy, curly, and coily hair textures.

What makes MELD different

Waterproof hold

The hold is rated for 48 hours and built to survive sweat, rain, and humidity. It is designed not to release until you choose to shampoo it out, so it holds through a workout or a long day outside.

Undetectable finish

The fibers are designed to read as real hair in normal indoor and outdoor light, with no chalky residue, flat patches, or obvious coverage line.

Clean and reversible

The formulation is non-comedogenic and mineral-pigment based with rosemary oil, ginseng extract, and biotin. It washes out cleanly with any shampoo and leaves no buildup.

Honest fit check

A density filler needs hair to bond to. If you have thinning areas with hair still present, it works well. If an area is completely bald, the fibers have nothing to grip and the look is less convincing. We would rather qualify you honestly than sell to the wrong customer.
